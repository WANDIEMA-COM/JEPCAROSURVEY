How Our Land Survey Process Works

Clear, step-by-step procedure to ensure your land boundaries are legally sound.

1

Consultation & Search

We review your title deed copy, registry index map (RIM), or deed plan to verify land details before field operations.

2

Site Fieldwork

Our survey team visits the ground using high-precision RTK GNSS equipment to locate or place concrete beacons accurately.

3

Plan Drafting

Survey computations are processed, generating mutation forms, topographical CAD models, or sectional plans.

4

Approval & Handover

Final authenticated survey plans are submitted to County/Director of Surveys, and certified documentation delivered to you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries about land surveying in Kenya.

Beacon re-establishment for standard residential plots (e.g., 50x100 or 100x100) generally ranges from KES 15,000 to KES 35,000 depending on location, availability of Registry Index Maps (RIM), and terrain. Contact us with your parcel number for an exact quote.
A Mutation Form is used for land under the Registered Land Act (general boundaries, such as agricultural or residential plots with RIMs). A Deed Plan is prepared for fixed survey boundaries (often commercial land or leaseholds authenticated directly by the Survey of Kenya).
A licensed surveyor confirms that the plot shown to you by the seller matches the exact coordinates on government maps. This prevents buying road reserves, public utility land, or neighboring plots.
Standard subdivision typically takes between 2 to 6 weeks, depending on County Planning approvals, Land Control Board (LCB) consent schedules, and registration at the Ministry of Lands.
